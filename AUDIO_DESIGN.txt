ODY.MONSTER — AUDIO DESIGN

The site deliberately does NOT play every uploaded sound at once.

The warning/ENTER click starts the entire sound engine and unlocks browser audio.
From that point the layers are scheduled independently so the visitor cannot easily
learn the pattern.

BASE:
- ambient-a / ambient-b: two 60-second dark-ambient excerpts, crossfaded continuously.

WHISPER LAYER:
- four voices with echo: spectral swarm / far voices.
- 27-second male whisper: low and close/far fragments.
- reversed whispers: unstable transitional texture.

IMAGE MICRO-CUES:
Every image may receive a different tiny cue: breath at the ear, a fragment of whisper,
teeth, violin scrape, low bass movement, or occasionally no obvious transient.
This avoids the repeated 'same screech every image' effect.

RARE WORLD EVENTS:
- demonic moan: distant low presence.
- suffocating man: filtered and reverberant, short fragments.
- dragging metal: far spatial intrusion.
- bells: long, very quiet ominous layer.
- distant distorted scream: repaired from left-only to stereo, heavily lowered and reverberated.
- crying: strongly lowered, filtered and reverberated to behave like distant weeping.

CHOIR:
- creepy multi-voice choir: 3–6 second incursions, often starting from the middle.
- horror choir transition: short punctuation only and used rarely.

DYNAMICS:
The page periodically enters a short escalation state, then a deliberate lull.
A master compressor prevents overlapping random events from suddenly summing into
an excessively loud output.
